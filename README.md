# Multi-Stage Packed-Bed Water Filtration Column

An experimental 4-stage packed-bed filtration column designed to evaluate physical depth filtration, mass balance recovery, and hydraulic flow dynamics across sequential loading trials.

---

## Technical Highlights
* **Flow Rate Performance:** Achieved a steady-state volumetric flow rate of Q = 2.07mL/s.
* **Liquid Retention Analysis:** Documented a initial 34.0mL 13.6% liquid retention loss during pre-trial due to dry media saturation, achieving 95% volumetric recovery in subsequent runs.
* **Design Iteration:** Redesigned bottom outlet geometry and integrated a media pre-flushing phase to reduce effluent turbidity and eliminate pressure buildup.

---

## Column Architecture (Top to Bottom)
1. **Gravel Layer:** Primary coarse straining for large suspended solids.
2. **Fine Sand Layer:** Secondary physical straining and depth filtration.
3. **Activated Carbon Layer:** Chemical adsorption of dissolved contaminants and fine particulate removal.
4. **Cotton Base:** Final barrier to prevent media wash-through.

## Experimental Limitations
* **Manual Data Collection:** Outflow rate Q = 2.07 mL/s and volume recovery were measured using manual timing and standard graduated containers rather than automated flow sensors.
* **Human Reaction Time Margin:** Manual stopwatch actuation introduces a timing variance of approximately +-0.5s per trial.
* **Visual Volume Reading:** Volumetric recovery carries a visual reading uncertainty of +- 5mL due to measuring cup graduation limits.
